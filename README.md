# Intenter

Intenter is supposed to help you be mindful of how long you use for mobile device for, and for what purpose.   

It aims to accomplish this by asking you what you you need your phone for and then logs the time and the apps you use in a session. The session ends when the screen is locked again. It is designed to bring a bit of friction into the habit of mindless scrolling and asks you to reconsider whether you really want to use your phone.   

The app does not connect to the internet but does want the permissions to start up automatically at every unlock event.   

Happy Disconnecting!
